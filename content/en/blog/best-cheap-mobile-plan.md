> **In brief:**
> 1. Free remains the reference for cheap plans: 2 euros per month for a backup line (free with a Freebox), 12.99 euros for 110 GB of 5G, 19.99 euros for 350 GB with a price frozen until 2027.
> 2. The low-cost brands B&You (5G from 9.99 euros), RED by SFR (150 GB 5G at 11.99 euros) and Sosh (200 GB at 15.99 euros) are all contract-free and compete on international use and price stability.
> 3. The number one trap is the increase after 12 months: read the "then X euros per month" line before comparing.

A cheap mobile plan in France in 2026 costs between 2 and 20 euros per month, with 5G almost everywhere and a data allowance far beyond what an average user consumes. Competition comes down to three points: the **price after the promotional period**, the **data usable in Europe and abroad**, and the **included services** (eSIM, TV, VPN, smartwatch). Here is the comparison of offers recorded on 13 September 2026.

## 1. Free 2 euro plan: the unbeatable backup

The **Free 2 euro plan** has existed since Free Mobile launched and has never changed price. It includes **2 hours of calls**, **unlimited texts and MMS** and **50 MB of data**. For a Freebox subscriber, it drops to **0 euro per month**. It is the ideal plan for a child, a second line, an elderly person who does not use internet on their phone, or a connected alarm. It does not suit daily data use: 50 MB is used up in a few minutes of browsing.

## 2. B&You: 5G from 9.99 euros

**B&You**, Bouygues Telecom's contract-free brand, is the only one to offer **5G at 9.99 euros per month**. The advantage lies in the network: Bouygues Telecom ranks first in the nPerf 2026 barometer on latency and web browsing, and first with ARCEP in dense urban areas. B&You's higher tiers add data and slightly more generous roaming than average. Check when subscribing whether the price is guaranteed beyond the first year: B&You's promotional tiers change often.

## 3. RED 150 GB: simplicity with no increase

The **RED by SFR 150 GB 5G plan at 11.99 euros per month** is one of the most readable offers on the market: **no scheduled increase**, no hidden conditions, a formula centred on data. The SFR network improved in the ARCEP 2025 measurements (87% of perfect calls) and SFR ranks first on mobile problem resolution (88%). It is the plan to pick for a stable price under 12 euros without being a box customer.

## 4. Série Free 110 GB: Free's introductory price

The **Série Free 110 GB 5G** costs **12.99 euros per month for 12 months**, then switches to the Free 5G+ 350 GB plan at 19.99 euros. It is the entry point to Free Mobile for full use. The switch is explicit and goes to a more generous plan, which sets it apart from promotions that renew the same allowance at a higher price.

## 5. Sosh Voyage 200 GB: the Orange network for travellers

**Sosh** gives access to the **Orange network**, ARCEP's best mobile network for the fifteenth consecutive year, without a contract. The **Voyage 200 GB 5G plan at 15.99 euros per month** stands out for its international side, wider than average: Europe, French overseas departments and many destinations outside Europe. It is the cheap plan for frequent travellers and for people in rural areas, where Orange keeps a clear lead (83% call quality against 76% for Free).

## 6. Free 5G+ 350 GB plan: the reference at 19.99 euros

The **Free 5G+ 350 GB plan at 19.99 euros per month** is the most complete plan under 20 euros. The price is **frozen until 2027**, and the offer regularly gains included services: **eSIM for a smartwatch**, **Free mVPN**, **more than 300 TV channels** in the app. Freebox subscribers get up to 10 euros off per month. Free is also the operator with the widest 5G coverage according to nPerf, via the 700 MHz band.

## 7. Free Max plan: unlimited for travellers

Launched on 31 March 2026, the **Free Max plan** at **29.99 euros per month** (19.99 euros for Freebox subscribers, up to four plans) offers **unlimited 5G in France**, **unlimited internet in more than 135 destinations** and unlimited calls and texts from more than 65 countries. It leaves the "cheap" bracket at full price, but becomes the cheapest unlimited plan on the market for a Freebox subscriber.

## The three traps of cheap plans

- **The increase after 12 months.** A promotion at 9.99 or 12.99 euros is worth what its month-13 price is worth. The RED 150 GB, Sosh Voyage 200 GB and Free 5G+ plans have none.
- **Useless data.** An average user consumes between 15 and 30 GB per month. Paying for 350 GB only makes sense for tethering or video on the move.
- **The network.** A cheap plan on a network that does not reach your home is a bad plan. ARCEP's coverage maps (monreseaumobile.arcep.fr) let you check before subscribing.
