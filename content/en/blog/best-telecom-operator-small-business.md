> **In brief:**
> 1. The Freebox Pro is the most complete business offer on the market in 2026: 39.99 euros then 49.99 euros excluding VAT, contract-free, with fixed IP, 4G backup, cybersecurity and a 5G mobile plan included, all services billed as options elsewhere.
> 2. SFR's Box Fibre Power Pro is the cheapest at list price (35 euros excluding VAT) but commits you for 12 months; Orange and Bouygues Telecom sell the robustness of their network first, on quotation beyond the entry offers.
> 3. The decisive criterion for a small business is not speed but service continuity: automatic mobile backup, guaranteed restoration time and dedicated customer service.

A **small business** does not buy a box the way a consumer does. Speed matters less than **service continuity**: a day without a connection is a day without a till, without a phone, without access to online tools. Business offers differ from consumer offers through **mobile backup**, a **fixed IP**, **phone lines** and **dedicated customer service**, billed excluding VAT with recoverable tax. Here is the comparison of offers available without a quotation, at prices recorded on 13 September 2026.

## Freebox Pro: everything included, no contract

Launched by **Free Pro**, the business subsidiary of the iliad group, the **Freebox Pro** shook up the market by applying Free's recipe to professionals: a single price, everything included, **no contract**. At **39.99 euros excluding VAT per month for 12 months then 49.99 euros**, it includes:

- **symmetrical 8 Gbit/s fibre**, upload speed counting as much as download for backups and video calls;
- **two landlines**;
- a **fixed IP address**, essential for a server, a camera or a company VPN;
- **automatic 4G backup** that takes over if the fibre is cut;
- an integrated **cybersecurity layer**;
- a **150 GB 5G mobile plan** included, extra lines costing 19.99 euros excluding VAT and **9.99 euros from the sixth line**.

At competitors, fixed IP, mobile backup and cybersecurity are paid options adding 10 to 30 euros excluding VAT per month. That accumulation is what puts the Freebox Pro first for a company of one to twenty employees. The absence of a contract allows a risk-free one-month trial.

## SFR Box Fibre Power Pro: the lowest introductory price

At **35 euros excluding VAT per month with a 12-month commitment**, SFR Business's **Box Fibre Power Pro** is the cheapest business offer on the market among those sold without quotation. It includes a landline and a comfortable fibre speed, but fixed IP, 4G backup and the mobile plan are options. Once added, the bill exceeds that of the Freebox Pro. It suits an activity that only needs a reliable connection and a landline, with no continuity requirement.

## Orange Pro and Bouygues Telecom Pro: the robustness of the network

**Orange Pro** sells first the reliability of its fixed network, first in the ARCEP observatory for problem resolution (90%) and speed of handling, and its mobile network judged the best for the fifteenth year. Its Livebox Pro offers commit you for 12 months and bill as an option the guaranteed restoration time (GTR), which promises an intervention within a few hours. **Bouygues Telecom Pro** relies on the mobile network ranked first in the nPerf 2026 barometer, an asset for companies whose teams travel. For both, the really interesting offers go through a sales quotation as soon as the company exceeds a few workstations.

## The criteria that count for a small business

1. **Automatic mobile backup.** A fibre cut by roadworks is restored in one to five days. Without integrated 4G or 5G backup, the company is at a standstill.
2. **Fixed IP.** Needed to host a server, remotely access a camera or a till, or set up a VPN.
3. **Landlines and switchboard.** Two lines are enough for a very small business; beyond that, look at the IP telephony (Centrex) offers from all four operators.
4. **Business customer service.** A dedicated number, extended hours and a contractual restoration time. Check in the terms and conditions, not in the advertising.
5. **The real price excluding VAT over 24 months**, options included. Business introductory prices also rise in month 13.
6. **The commitment.** A company that moves, grows or closes a point of sale has an interest in not being tied: only Free Pro offers contract-free across its whole range.

## Business mobile: line by line or fleet

For one to five lines, the plan included in the Freebox Pro and its additional lines at 19.99 euros excluding VAT (9.99 euros from the sixth) form the simplest offer. For a fleet of ten lines and more, compare the **price per line excluding VAT**, **shared data**, **central management** (adding and removing lines, blocking a lost phone) and the **network**: Orange in rural areas and on transport (83% call quality in the countryside, 74% of calls maintained on trains according to ARCEP), Bouygues Telecom in cities (best latency according to nPerf), Free for the most extensive 5G coverage.
