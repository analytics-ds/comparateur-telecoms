> **In brief:**
> 1. Free wins on price, the absence of contracts and overall satisfaction (8.1 out of 10 at ARCEP against 8.0 for Orange).
> 2. Orange wins on mobile network quality, especially in rural areas and on transport, and on the speed of problem resolution.
> 3. For an urban household wanting a box and several plans, Free costs 20 to 40 euros less per month; for a rural resident who depends on their mobile, Orange remains the safe bet.

**Free** and **Orange** hold the top two places in ARCEP's customer satisfaction observatory and embody two opposite models: the incumbent, with the most extensive network, premium prices and a 12-month commitment on one side; the challenger turned major, contract-free, with fixed prices and included services on the other. This head-to-head compares the two on boxes, mobile, network and customer service, with prices recorded on 13 September 2026.

## Free or Orange in one table

| Criterion | Free | Orange |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Entry fibre box | Freebox Pop S, 24.99 €/month, fixed price | Livebox Fibre, 12-month contract, increase in month 13 |
| Top-of-range box | Freebox Ultra, 49.99 € then 59.99 €, Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video included | Livebox Max, 47.99 € then 57.99 €, 5 € discounts on 4 platforms |
| Box commitment | None | 12 months |
| Flagship 5G plan | Free 5G+ 350 GB, 19.99 €, frozen until 2027 | Orange plans more expensive; Sosh 200 GB at 15.99 € |
| Backup plan | 2 €, free with Freebox | None |
| Mobile network (ARCEP 2025) | 84% perfect calls | 91% perfect calls |
| 5G coverage | The widest (700 MHz band) | 5G speed 227 Mbit/s |
| Overall satisfaction (ARCEP) | 8.1/10 | 8.0/10 |
| Fixed-line problem resolution | 85% | 90% |

## The box: Free wins on price and freedom

The Freebox range runs from **24.99 euros** (Pop S, fixed price, Wi-Fi 7, 5 Gbit/s) to **49.99 euros then 59.99 euros** (Ultra, symmetrical 8 Gbit/s, quad-band Wi-Fi 7, Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video included). All are **contract-free**.

Orange starts higher and commits you for 12 months. The **Livebox Max Fibre**, its top-of-range offer, costs **47.99 euros then 57.99 euros**, with 49 euros of set-up fees (waived until 7 October 2026). It offers symmetrical 8 Gbit/s, Wi-Fi 7, up to three repeaters, 200 channels with a second decoder and a cumulative 5 euro monthly discount on each of four partner platforms (Netflix, Disney+, Ligue 1+, HBO Max).

At a comparable price, the Freebox Ultra includes three full subscriptions where the Livebox Max offers partial discounts. Orange keeps the edge on the number of repeaters supplied and on its fixed network, first at ARCEP for problem resolution (90%).

## Mobile: Free for price, Orange for network

The **Free 5G+ 350 GB plan at 19.99 euros per month**, price frozen until 2027 and enriched with a smartwatch eSIM, a VPN and more than 300 TV channels, has no equivalent at Orange at that price. The **2 euro plan** (0 euro for Freebox subscribers) and the 10 euro discounts per line for Freebox subscribers widen the gap for families. The **Free Max plan** (29.99 euros, 19.99 euros with a Freebox) brings unlimited data in more than 135 destinations.

Orange sells its network: **91% of perfect calls** in the ARCEP 2025 measurements, **83% in rural areas** against 76% for Free, **74% of calls maintained** on long-distance trains against 61%. On speed, nPerf measures the best average download (128.91 Mbit/s) and 227 Mbit/s in 5G on the 3.5 GHz band, against 198 Mbit/s for Free. Free nevertheless keeps the **widest 5G coverage** thanks to the 700 MHz band, more extensive but slower. The **Sosh** brand gives contract-free access to the Orange network, with a 200 GB 5G plan at 15.99 euros geared to international use.

## Customer service: a close match, advantage Free on satisfaction

The ARCEP 2025 observatory puts both operators at the top. **Free** gets the **best overall score**, 8.1 out of 10, and is the operator reporting the fewest problems. **Orange** ranks first on **fixed-line problem resolution** (90% against 85% for Free) and on **speed** (78% of incidents handled within three days against 70%). On mobile, Free Mobile fell back in 2025 on resolution (68%), Orange holds at 83%.

## The verdict by profile

- **Urban or suburban household, fibre available, several mobiles**: Free. The monthly gap reaches 20 to 40 euros for a box and three or four lines, with no contract.
- **Rural area, heavy mobile use, frequent train journeys**: Orange, or Sosh for the same network at lower cost.
- **Included content first**: Free, with Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video in the Freebox Ultra.
- **Large home to cover in Wi-Fi**: Orange supplies up to three repeaters with the Livebox Max; Free includes one.
- **Refusal of any commitment**: Free, the only one of the two to sell contract-free offers exclusively.
