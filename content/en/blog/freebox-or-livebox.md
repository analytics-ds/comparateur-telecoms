> **In brief:**
> 1. The Freebox wins on price (Pop S at a fixed 24.99 euros), the absence of contracts and included content (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video on the Ultra).
> 2. The Livebox Max wins on Wi-Fi coverage of large homes (up to three repeaters) and on Orange's fixed-line service, first at ARCEP on problem resolution.
> 3. At an equal budget, the Freebox Ultra offers more than the Livebox Max; the difference comes down to commitment and home size.

Free's **Freebox** and Orange's **Livebox** are the two best-selling box ranges in France. They look alike on paper (fibre up to 8 Gbit/s, Wi-Fi 7, 4K TV) and differ on everything else: pricing model, commitment, content strategy and the way they cover a home in Wi-Fi. This comparison relies on public prices recorded on 13 September 2026.

## Price: the Freebox pulls ahead at entry level

Free is the only one to offer a Wi-Fi 7 fibre box at a **fixed price**, the **Freebox Pop S at 24.99 euros per month**. Orange has no equivalent: the entry-level Livebox Fibre is sold with a 12-month commitment and an increase in month 13. Over 24 months, a household saves several hundred euros by choosing the Pop S over an entry-level Livebox.

At the top of the range, prices converge: **Freebox Ultra at 49.99 euros then 59.99 euros**, **Livebox Max at 47.99 euros then 57.99 euros**. At Orange you must add **49 euros of set-up fees** (waived in a promotion until 7 October 2026) and the **12-month commitment**.

## Content: three full subscriptions against four discounts

The **Freebox Ultra includes Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video** at no extra cost. The **Livebox Max** offers a **5 euro monthly discount, cumulative, on each of four partner platforms**: Netflix, Disney+, Ligue 1+ and HBO Max. A household taking the three platforms included at Free saves the list price of three subscriptions; at Orange, it saves 15 euros on three subscriptions it pays for anyway. The Freebox advantage is clear. The Livebox Max keeps a card for football fans with the Ligue 1+ discount, absent at Free.

## Wi-Fi: quad-band on the Freebox Ultra, repeater count on the Livebox Max

The **Freebox Ultra** uses **quad-band Wi-Fi 7**, the most advanced configuration on the market, with **one repeater included**. The **Livebox Max** uses **Wi-Fi 7** with **up to three repeaters on request**. In a flat or a single-storey house, the Freebox Ultra covers without difficulty. In a large house on several floors, Orange's three repeaters make the difference, without buying extra equipment.

At entry level, the Freebox Pop S offers Wi-Fi 7 and a repeater on request where the Livebox Fibre stays on Wi-Fi 6 with an optional repeater.

## TV: 4K Player against Orange decoder

The **Freebox Pop** ships with the **Free 4K HDR TV Player** and more than 300 channels; the Ultra Essentiel and Ultra go up to 340 channels. The **Livebox Max** offers **200 channels** with a **second decoder included**, useful for a household with two televisions. The Freebox Pop S, for its part, uses the Oqee app on a connected TV, with no set-top box.

## Fixed network and customer service

Orange ranks first in the ARCEP 2025 observatory on **fixed-line problem resolution** (90% against 85% for Free) and on **speed of handling** (78% of incidents resolved within three days against 70%). Free gets the **best overall satisfaction** (8.1 out of 10 against 8.0) and reports the fewest problems. Both are well ahead of Bouygues Telecom and SFR.

## The verdict

- **Controlled budget, flat, no commitment**: Freebox Pop S.
- **Household already paying for Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video**: Freebox Ultra, which repays its price difference through the included subscriptions.
- **Large house on several floors, two televisions**: Livebox Max, for its three repeaters and second decoder.
- **Loyal to the Orange fixed network**: Livebox Max, while waiting for the end of the promotional period to renegotiate.

## Method and sources

Prices recorded on 13 September 2026 on free.fr and boutique.orange.fr, cross-checked with Selectra, Echos du Net and Edcom. Satisfaction: [ARCEP observatory](https://www.arcep.fr/cartes-et-donnees/nos-publications-chiffrees/satisfaction-client/observatoire-de-la-satisfaction-client-edition-2026.html).
