> **In brief:**
> 1. Call 3179 to get your RIO code, give it to the new operator when subscribing: it is the one that cancels the old contract.
> 2. Porting is free, takes at most three working days and only cuts the line for a few hours on porting day.
> 3. The only possible fees come from the old operator: remaining months of a commitment or cancellation fees, often refunded by the new operator on proof.

Switching mobile operator in France in 2026 takes ten minutes of paperwork and three days of waiting. **Number portability** is a right guaranteed by the postal and electronic communications code: the number belongs to the subscriber, not to the operator. The system relies on a single code, the **RIO**, and on a simple principle: the new operator does all the work. Here is the step-by-step procedure, the real timescales and the fees to anticipate.

## The procedure in four steps

### 1. Get your RIO code on 3179

The **RIO** (Relevé d'Identité Opérateur) is a **12-character** code specific to each line. You get it by calling **3179** from the phone concerned. This voice server is **free**, **shared by every operator** and available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**. The code is read out then sent by text message with the end date of any commitment.

Asking for your RIO **commits you to nothing**: it triggers no cancellation and stays valid as long as the line is active. The RIO also appears in most operators' customer areas.

### 2. Subscribe with the new operator using the RIO

When ordering the new plan, online or in a shop, choose the **"keep my number"** option and enter the RIO. The new operator checks the code, proposes a **porting date** (at the earliest three working days later, or later if the subscriber wishes, for example to wait for the end of a commitment) and takes charge of **cancelling the old contract**. No letter to send, no call to make to the old operator.

### 3. Receive the new SIM or activate the eSIM

The SIM card arrives by post within two to five days, or the eSIM is available immediately in the new operator's app. Do not insert it before porting day: the old line keeps working until then.

### 4. On the day, switch over

**Porting takes four hours at most**, usually in the early morning. As soon as the old SIM loses signal, insert the new one or activate the eSIM, then restart the phone. The number is kept as it was; contacts, apps and messaging services do not move.

## Real timescales

| Step | Time |
|------|------|
| Getting the RIO on 3179 | Immediate |
| Subscription and RIO check | A few minutes |
| Number porting | 3 working days at most, or chosen date |
| Service interruption on the day | 4 hours at most |
| Receiving a physical SIM | 2 to 5 days |
| Final invoice from the old operator | Within the following month |

## Fees to anticipate

Porting itself is **free**. Fees come only from the old contract:

- **Remaining months of a commitment**: beyond month 12 of a 24-month commitment, the Chatel law caps the amount due at 25% of the remaining monthly payments. Before month 12, the whole amount is due. Contract-free plans (Free Mobile, B&You, RED by SFR, Sosh) do not raise the question.
- **Cancellation fees**: rare on mobile, more common on boxes. The text message received after calling 3179 states the commitment end date.
- **Refund by the new operator**: several operators refund all or part of the old contract's cancellation fees on presentation of the final invoice. Ask when subscribing.
- **Subsidised handset**: a phone bought with a contract plan may still be owed; check the old contract's terms.

## The mistakes that waste time

- **Cancelling yourself before porting the number.** A cancelled number can no longer be ported. Always let the new operator cancel via the RIO.
- **Inserting the new SIM too early.** It only works from the porting onwards; the old one cuts out at the same moment.
- **Calling 3179 from the wrong line.** The RIO is specific to each number; to port several lines, call 3179 from each.
- **Forgetting the commitment end date.** It is stated in the 3179 text message; choosing a porting date just after it avoids paying the remaining months.
- **Confusing mobile RIO and landline RIO.** A landline's RIO is obtained on 3179 from the landline or in the box customer area; the two portings are independent.
