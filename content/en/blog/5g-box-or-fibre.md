> **In brief:**
> 1. Fibre remains the best choice wherever available: stable speed, low latency, equal or lower price (Freebox Pop S at 24.99 euros).
> 2. Without fibre, the 5G box has replaced ADSL and the 4G box: six operators offer one, from 24.99 euros (RED) to 42.99 euros (Orange), all contract-free and with no technician.
> 3. The Free 5G box (29.99 euros then 39.99 euros, Wi-Fi 7, 1.5 Gbit/s, TV included) is the most complete; the RED Box 5G the cheapest.

In 2026 the question no longer arises for households connected to fibre: a fibre box is faster, more stable and often cheaper than a 5G box. It arises for **homes not yet connected**, **areas where connection is delayed**, **short lets** and **second homes**. For them, the **5G box** has replaced ADSL and the 4G box. Here is what the offers recorded on 13 September 2026 are worth, and in which cases they hold up against fibre.

## Fibre or 5G: the real differences

**Theoretical speed** is comparable: 1 to 1.5 Gbit/s for a 5G box, 1 to 8 Gbit/s for fibre. **Real speed** is not. Fibre delivers its speed whatever the time of day; 5G shares the mast's capacity with every connected user and varies with distance, walls and weather. A household 300 metres from a 3.5 GHz mast will get several hundred Mbit/s; a household at the edge of 700 MHz coverage, a few dozen.

**Latency** is the second gap: a few milliseconds on fibre, 20 to 40 ms on 5G at best (30.15 ms on average for Bouygues Telecom, the best, according to nPerf). Remote work and streaming do not notice it; competitive online gaming does.

**Upload speed** clearly favours fibre: 150 Mbit/s upload on the RED Box 5G, against symmetrical 5 to 8 Gbit/s on the best fibre offers. That is the criterion to watch for anyone sending large files or streaming live.

**Installation** favours 5G: no technician, no work, no waiting. For a six-month let or a second home, that is decisive.

## Free 5G box: the most complete

The **Free 5G box** at **29.99 euros per month for 12 months then 39.99 euros**, contract-free, stands out for its equipment: **Wi-Fi 7**, speeds up to **1.5 Gbit/s** and **more than 300 TV channels included**. It is the only 5G box that reproduces the experience of a full fibre box. It relies on Free's 5G network, which according to nPerf is the most extensive in France thanks to the 700 MHz band, which matters for a home in a sparsely populated area: better a 200 Mbit/s 5G that is available than a 230 Mbit/s 5G that is absent.

## RED Box 5G: the cheapest and the most price-stable

At **24.99 euros per month, contract-free and with no increase**, the **RED Box 5G** is the price floor among the major operators. It offers 1.1 Gbit/s download and 150 Mbit/s upload, without included TV. It is the rational choice for a household that streams its content and only wants a connection.

## SFR, Bouygues Telecom and Orange: network before price

The **SFR Box 5G** (32.99 euros) and the **Bbox 5G** (34.99 euros) rely on their networks: Bouygues Telecom shows the best 5G speed measured by nPerf (231 Mbit/s on the 3.5 GHz band) and the best latency. Orange's **Box 5G Home** (42.99 euros) is the most expensive, with speeds up to 1.5 Gbit/s and access to the network judged best by ARCEP, which matters in rural areas where Orange keeps 83% call quality against 76% for Free.

## The 4G box has almost no reason left to exist

Bouygues Telecom, SFR and Free have **discontinued their 4G boxes**. Orange is the last major operator still selling one, the **Box 4G Home**. The 4G box only keeps an interest in areas with no 5G coverage at all, which shrink every month. Everywhere else, the 5G box is barely more expensive for three to five times the speed.

## How to decide

1. **Test fibre eligibility** at the address on any operator's site. If fibre is available, take it: the Freebox Pop S at a fixed 24.99 euros costs less than any 5G box.
2. **Check each operator's 5G coverage** on monreseaumobile.arcep.fr, distinguishing 3.5 GHz 5G (fast) from 700 MHz or 2.1 GHz 5G (extensive).
3. **Use the absence of a contract**: order the 5G box, test it for a week where it will be installed, return it if the speed disappoints.
4. **Place the box near a window** facing the nearest mast: the speed gain can double.
